Our vision

We want to make reuse the first choice

The most sustainable garment or piece of furniture is the one that already exists. With Tradeo you search for exactly what you want, when it suits you, and swap with people all over Norway. As easy as buying new.

What we stand for

Sustainability that works

Every swap gives a thing a longer life, so something new does not have to be made.

Inspiring reuse

Pre-loved can be just as nice as new. Style is about how you put things together.

Room for everyone

For everyone, whatever your body, gender, background, ability or budget.

Honest and safe

The top-up is held safely, and we crack down on fraud and harassment.

Tradeocoins

Fair swaps, even when the values don’t quite match

If the thing you want is worth a little more than what you offer, you add Tradeocoins. The person with the more valuable item receives them and can use them on something else in the app.

Tradeocoins cannot be exchanged back into money. The top-up in a single swap can be at most 1,000 Tradeocoins.

Example
Your jumper300
+
Tradeocoins150
=
Ingrid’s loafers450

Ingrid gets 150 Tradeocoins to spend on something else she likes.

Terms of use

The terms apply to the use of the Tradeo app. You see the full text in the app before you create an account, and it will be published here when the app launches. These are the main points. The Norwegian version of the terms is the one that applies.

Who can use Tradeo

You must be at least 18. Tradeo is for private individuals: you can swap things you have used yourself or no longer need, but not resell goods or swap on behalf of a shop.

What it costs

Creating an account, listing items, sending messages and completing swaps are all free.

Swaps

When you agree on a swap, the agreement is between the two of you. You agree on condition, time and place of handover, and any shipping, yourselves.

Tradeocoins and top-ups

  • Tradeocoins even out differences in value in swaps, and can never be exchanged back into money.
  • The top-up is locked when the swap is accepted, and released when both of you have confirmed receipt.
  • If you don’t confirm within 10 days of delivery, the swap is treated as completed.
  • The top-up in a single swap can be at most 1,000 Tradeocoins.

What you can’t list

Stolen or counterfeit goods, weapons, drugs, alcohol and tobacco, prescription medicines, live animals and anything else that is illegal or dangerous. Luxury items are checked before they are shown to others.

If something goes wrong

Report it in the app before the deadlines run out. The swap is then locked, and we look into it. If you disagree with a decision we have made, you can appeal to hjelp@tradeoapp.com within 6 months.

Questions about the terms?

Email us at hjelp@tradeoapp.com or use the support form. See also the website privacy notice.
